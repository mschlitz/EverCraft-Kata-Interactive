## Credits

This project is a tribute to the **EverCraft Kata**, created by Guy Royse [(@guyroyse)](https://twitter.com/guyroyse) and George Walters II [(@walterg2)](https://twitter.com/walterg2).

All of the kata's content and the EverCraft logo come from their original work, and this repository is a fork of it:

**[PuttingTheDnDInTDD/EverCraft-Kata](https://github.com/PuttingTheDnDInTDD/EverCraft-Kata)**

> May all your rolls be 20s.

## What this is

An interactive web page that presents the same overall contents as the original kata (every iteration and feature) with real checkboxes so you can track your progress as you work through it.

- Check off requirements as you complete them, with progress bars per iteration and overall
- Take notes on each feature
- Export your progress and notes to Markdown
- Progress and notes are saved in your browser (localStorage)

**[Try it here →](https://mschlitz.github.io/EverCraft-Kata-Interactive/)**

Or open [`index.html`](index.html) locally in a browser.
